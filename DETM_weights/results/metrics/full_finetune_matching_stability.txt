KEY FINDINGS
====================================================================================================

Post-hoc matching suffers from:
1. Temporal instability - topics match to different topics over time
2. Ambiguous matches - multiple plausible matches with similar scores
3. No guaranteed semantic consistency across corpora
4. Matching is done independently at each time slice

This demonstrates the superiority of built-in shared topic space where:
- Topics are aligned by construction
- Temporal consistency is guaranteed
- Matches are deterministic and stable
